My story

From Dover, Delaware to Tuscaloosa, Alabama — learning to embrace change and make the most of new opportunities.

Introduction

My name is Ava Jensen, and I am a student at the University of Alabama majoring in finance. Originally from Dover, Delaware, moving to Alabama has given me the opportunity to experience a new environment, meet new people, and become more independent. College has encouraged me to step outside my comfort zone while discovering new interests and opportunities. I hope to build a strong foundation that prepares me for a meaningful career and continued personal growth.

A personal turning point

One experience that has shaped me is moving away from my hometown in Delaware to attend college in Alabama. Starting over in a new environment has taught me the importance of independence, adaptability, and being willing to embrace change. It has encouraged me to take initiative, meet new people, and become more confident in my decisions. I have learned that growth often comes from unfamiliar situations, and that lesson influences how I approach my education, relationships, and future goals.

Interests & passions

I enjoy experiences that allow me to learn, grow, and connect with others. I am interested in finance and investing, exploring new places, personal development, and building meaningful friendships and professional relationships. These interests reflect my curiosity, willingness to try new things, and desire to keep developing as a person.

02 · Career goals

Building a future in finance.

My goal is to turn what I learn in the classroom into knowledge and experience that I can carry into a career.

Where I hope to go

As a finance major, I am especially interested in wealth management and financial services. I enjoy learning how financial markets work, how investments are managed, and how financial decisions can influence long-term goals. Finance combines analytical thinking, problem-solving, and relationship-building — areas I hope to continue developing.

“I want to build a career grounded in knowledge, trust, and continuous growth.”
Short-term goals

Build my foundation

  • Maintain strong academic performance while completing my finance degree.
  • Strengthen my understanding of investing, financial markets, and wealth management.
  • Improve technical skills, especially Excel and financial analysis.
  • Connect with finance professionals through career fairs, networking, and LinkedIn.
  • Explore student organizations, internships, and other hands-on opportunities.
Long-term goals

Grow into my career

  • Establish a career in wealth management or a related financial services field.
  • Continue developing my knowledge and taking on new responsibilities.
  • Build lasting, trusted relationships with colleagues and clients.
  • Become a knowledgeable and dependable finance professional.
  • Create a career that supports continued growth and a healthy work-life balance.
